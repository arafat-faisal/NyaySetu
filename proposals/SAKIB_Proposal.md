### **Project Name (Proposed Title)**

**"Nyay-Triage: AI-Powered Case Screening, Statutory Reporting & Legal Aid Workflow Dashboard"**

### **1\. "Smart Triage & Priority Engine" Aligned with the Legal Framework**

Usually, officers make decisions by manually reviewing files. In our system, as soon as scanned application forms or documents are uploaded, the AI will automatically determine priority across three levels in accordance with the law and policy guidelines:

#### **a. Red Alert / High Priority (Emergency Legal Support)**

Pursuant to **Section 7(1)(c)** of the Act regarding "Emergency Legal Support" and **Paragraph 2(2) of the 2014 Policy**, the system will automatically assign **top priority** to the following cases:

1. **Detainees held without trial:** Paragraph 2(2)(dha) and referrals from prison authorities—those who are unable to arrange bail or defense due to financial constraints.  
2. **Martyr families and July Fighters of the July Mass Uprising:** Under Paragraph 2(2)(da), a priority group included in the new 2026 amendment.  
3. **Enforced disappeared persons or their affected families:** Special category under Paragraph 2(2)(dha).  
4. **Women and children victims of violence and trafficking:** Victims of human trafficking, acid attacks, or immediate physical/domestic violence (Paragraph 2(2)(b, c, jha, cha)).

#### **b. Medium Priority (Mandatory Mediation / ADR Track)**

* **Section 21B of the Act (Section 21B \- 2026 Amendment):** For disputes scheduled by the government, **"Mandatory Pre-case Mediation"** must be conducted at the Legal Aid Office prior to filing a lawsuit in court.  
* The system will analyze the application to determine whether it is a compoundable civil, family, or criminal matter (Section 21A(2)). If it is, instead of appointing a lawyer for court litigation, it will directly route it to the **"ADR Track."**

#### **c. Regular Priority (Standard Legal Aid & Consultation)**

* Other applicants whose annual income is below the government income tax threshold (Policy Paragraph 2(1)(a)) or general legal advice seekers (legal advice is open to all under Paragraph 3).

### **2\. Official Form Automation (Data Entry & Forms Generation)**

Currently, legal aid office staff waste a significant amount of time manually writing various registers and monthly reports. In the backend, our dashboard will automatically generate official statutory forms directly from the case database:

1. **LA Forms 20 & 21 (Legal Advice Register and Report):**  
   * Who came to the office for advice (male/female), and what action was taken post-consultation (ADR/litigation/no action)—this will automatically be recorded in Form-20 and converted into a Form-21 summary report at the end of the month.  
2. **LA Form-22 (ADR Register):**  
   * Information on pre-case or post-case parties, dispute type (civil/criminal/family), mediation session dates, settlement amounts recovered in installments, and the count of male/female/child beneficiaries will be automatically cataloged per Form-22 specifications.  
3. **LA Forms 23 & 25 (Monthly Activity Report & Statistics):**  
   * The success/failure rate of ADR for the entire month, the number of withdrawn cases, and the total count of beneficiaries will be exported with a single click into **Form LA-23 and Form LA-25**, ready for immediate signature by the District and Sessions Judge.  
4. **Annual Confidential Report (Form LA-24 / ACR Metrics):**  
   * Officers' annual performance evaluations contain fields assessing ADR disposal and overall workload (Parts 3 and 5 of LA Form-24). The dashboard will feature performance analytics graphs for the officer, directly aiding the annual appraisal process.

### **3\. Smart Panel Lawyer Matching (Section 15 Compliant Matching)**

There are strict statutory requirements under **Section 15** of the Act and associated rules regarding the appointment of panel lawyers. Our algorithm will recommend lawyers while strictly complying with these rules:

1. **Experience Requirement:** Must have at least 5 years of practice experience in the District Court (Section 15(2)).  
2. **Female Lawyer Quota (Gender Balance):** Under Section 15(3), there is a mandate to maintain at least **one-third (1/3) female lawyers** on the panel. The system will prioritize female lawyers for female victims.  
3. **Litigant's Preference:** Pursuant to Section 15(4), if the justice-seeker expresses a preference for a specific lawyer, the system will take it into account.  
4. **Workload Balancing:** By tracking **LA Form-10** (Quarterly Report of Panel Lawyers), the system will assess how many active government cases each lawyer is currently handling to ensure an equitable distribution of work.

### **4\. 1-Page Structured Case Summary (Bengali AI Brief)**

For Legal Aid Officers, the AI will read lengthy FIRs, complaints, or petitions and generate the following structured summary:

* **Basic Information:** Name, address, occupation, and contact details of the applicant and opposite party.  
* **Facts in Brief:** Core facts or dispute summary (maximum 100–150 words).  
* **Applicable Legal Provisions & Case Nature:** Whether it is civil/criminal/family and the relevant statutory provisions.  
* **Eligibility Under Policy:** Under which sub-clause of the 2014 Policy the applicant qualifies for free legal aid (e.g., Paragraph 2(2)(dha) \- detained without trial).  
* **Proposed Action:**  
  * Does this route to mandatory ADR under Section 21B?  
  * Or will the applicant be assigned a panel lawyer for emergency legal aid under Section 7(1)(c)?  
* **Proposed Panel Lawyers:** Names and profiles of 3 suitable lawyers.

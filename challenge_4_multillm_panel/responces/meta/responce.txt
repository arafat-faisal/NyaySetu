### PART 2: ARCHITECTURAL, LEGAL & OPERATIONAL DEEP-DIVE

#### 1. Statutory and Ethical Framework: Why Maintenance Can Mediate, Dowry Cannot

Challenge 4 conflates two legally distinct claims requiring bifurcation.

**Claim A - Maintenance: Compoundable Civil Claim**
Under Family Courts Act 2023 Section 5, Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 Section 9, and Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act 2010 Section 16, maintenance for wife and 4-year-old child is a statutory entitlement to reasonable living expenses, food, medical and child support. It is financial, quantifiable, and classic ADR territory. Family Courts Act 2023 Section 14 itself mandates pre-trial reconciliation. Pre-case mediation under Legal Aid Services Act 2000 Section 21A aligns with this policy and is encouraged.

**Claim B - Dowry Motorcycle Demand: Non-Compoundable Criminal Offence**
Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3 makes demanding dowry directly or indirectly, here motorcycle backed by threat of second marriage, a cognizable, non-bailable offence punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment. Combined with nighttime battery under DV Act Section 3 economic abuse and physical abuse, and Penal Code Sections 323, 325, 506, this is human rights violation, not a bargainable civil term.

Mediation ethics under Bangladesh National Legal Aid Guidelines and UNODC standards on restorative justice prohibit mediating criminal coercion because:
- Free consent absent: Moyuri lives in husband family home, NID locked, call logs checked, 20-minute weekly window, cannot negotiate freely.
- Retaliatory danger: Engaging Sohel while victim remains trapped escalates risk of battery.
- State cannot negotiate a discount on crime: A Legal Aid Officer cannot compromise a Section 3 dowry offence.

System must therefore bifurcate: maintenance proceeds to ODR, dowry triggers mandatory halt.

#### 2. Low-Bandwidth Asynchronous Caucus Design

Video conferencing via Zoom or WebRTC is technically impossible with Kalai 2G drops and culturally impossible with surveillance. Design is PSTN voice-first and asynchronous.

**Tele-Caucus (Shuttle) Model:** Mediator never holds joint call. Separate audio sessions:
- Sohel in Gazipur: Scheduled during factory break on smartphone, IVR call in Bangla.
- Moyuri in Kalai: Conducted via Union Digital Center entrepreneur premises with female paralegal present, or during Friday safe window 12:45-13:15 via mother phone. No internet needed, only 2G voice.

Mediator shuttles offers. Issue narrowing document enforced digitally: only maintenance amount, due date 5th, arrears schedule, MFS wallet allowed. Dowry field locked.

This bridges 250 km dispersion, shift asymmetry, and hours-long network drops. Calls are recorded as voice tickets in DLAS, transcribed via Bangla ASR for record but decision remains human.

#### 4. Enforceability and MFS Accountability

Human-centric oversight remains final: DLAO is a serving judicial officer (Senior Assistant Judge) on deputation with powers under Legal Aid Services Act Section 21A.

**Outcome Formalization:**
Voice consent (Ripon saying "I consent" after TTS, Sohel pressing 1 on IVR, Moyuri thumbprint at UDC) is digitized. DLAO reviews digital record in Joypurhat and applies digital judicial signature under Section 21A(3) and 21A(4). Under Section 21A(4) and Code of Civil Procedure Section 89A, settlement has force of civil court decree, executable without fresh trial, with no appeal except fraud.

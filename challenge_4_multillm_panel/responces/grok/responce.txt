**PART 2: ARCHITECTURAL, LEGAL & OPERATIONAL DEEP-DIVE**

**1. Statutory & Ethical Framework**  
Maintenance for wife and minor child is a classic compoundable civil claim under Family Courts Act 2023 Sections 4–5, Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 Section 9 and Domestic Violence Act 2010 Section 16. It is therefore suitable for pre-case mediation under Legal Aid Services Act 2000 Section 21A and CPC Section 89A; the resulting settlement is executable as a decree.  

Dowry demand (motorcycle backed by second-marriage threats and prior battery) is a non-compoundable, cognizable offence under Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3. Domestic battery itself engages DV Act 2010 Section 3 and Penal Code offences. Bangladesh jurisprudence and international restorative-justice standards prohibit bargaining over criminal coercion or physical violence. Mediating such claims would invalidate consent, expose the victim to retaliation, and convert a criminal process into private compromise. Hence the hard stop.

**2. Low-Bandwidth Asynchronous Caucus Design**  
Synchronous video is impossible: Moyuri has no smartphone/internet, network drops for hours, Sohel works long factory shifts and both parties are tech-uncomfortable. The design therefore uses pure PSTN/IVR audio caucus (shuttle mediation). The mediator speaks separately to each party on ordinary cellular voice, scheduling around Gazipur shift breaks and Moyuri’s Friday safe window or a supervised UDC visit. This eliminates bandwidth failure, joint-confrontation risk and screen literacy barriers while still producing a structured, recorded negotiation trail.

**3. Zero-Screen Accessibility & Safety Protocol**  
Ripon’s blindness is solved by exclusive voice capture at 16699/UDC; no visual OTP, captcha or PDF is ever required. Screen-reader English menus are bypassed by human-assisted or TTS Bangla prompts. Moyuri’s surveillance is mitigated by: (a) registering only the mother’s number as contact, (b) absolute ban on unscheduled outbound SMS/calls to the button phone, (c) confining all interaction to the documented Friday window or a female-paralegal-supervised UDC session, and (d) proxy filing under DV Act Section 11 so Moyuri never needs to leave the household unescorted. Power-asymmetry screening is mandatory before any mediation invitation is issued to Sohel.

**4. Enforceability & MFS Accountability**  
Once parties consent, the DLAO (a judicial officer) reviews the digital record and applies a digital signature under Legal Aid Services Act Section 21A(4). The instrument thereby acquires the force of a civil/family-court decree under CPC Section 89A and is directly executable. Payment obligations are logged in DLAS and linked to bKash/Nagad/Rocket. Sohel remits against the case ID; funds route to the verified maternal MFS wallet. Ripon receives only a silent voice confirmation. Persistent default auto-generates an execution petition (jari mamla) in the Joypurhat Family Court, attaching Gazipur wages without requiring Moyuri to travel or instruct counsel. This closes the accountability loop while remaining entirely within existing statutory and mobile-money infrastructure.

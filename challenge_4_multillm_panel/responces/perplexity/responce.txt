## PART 1: OFFICIAL SUBMISSION RESPONSE

**Minimum digital process**

`Citizen entry -> Eligibility/intake -> Mediation -> Outcome -> Record/follow-up`  
`Safety risk detected -> STOP ODR -> Protection and criminal-justice referral`

**1. Citizen entry:** Ripon may call the legal-aid helpline 16699 or attend the Kalai Union Digital Center (UDC). A trained operator records his Bangla voice application, identifies him as Moyuri’s assisting representative, and creates a case number. No app, captcha, or readable document is required.

**2. Eligibility/intake:** A legal-aid officer verifies identity and basic eligibility by voice, records Moyuri’s safe Friday speaking window, and forbids unscheduled calls or SMS to her monitored phone. A female officer conducts a private safety interview. The intake separates the maintenance claim from the alleged motorcycle demand, threats, and battery.

**3. Mediation:** Only the maintenance and child-support issue proceeds. The DLAO conducts asynchronous, separate audio caucuses: Moyuri is contacted during her safe window through the UDC or a trusted safe number; Sohel is contacted during a scheduled Gazipur work break. The mediator shuttles proposals, explains rights in Bangla, and records voluntary consent. No joint video call is used.

**4. Outcome:** Terms specify monthly maintenance, child support, arrears, payment date, and a safe recipient account. The agreement is read aloud to Moyuri and Ripon; Sohel receives an audio readout. Consent is authenticated by witnessed thumbprint or voice confirmation, then reviewed and digitally signed by the DLAO under Section 21A of the Legal Aid Services Act 2000. The settlement is recorded with decree effect under that Act and Section 89A of the Code of Civil Procedure.

**5. Record/follow-up:** The secure case ledger stores consent, audio, attendance, agreement, and audit logs. bKash or Nagad payments use the case number; voice notifications avoid Moyuri’s unsafe phone. Missed payments trigger human follow-up and execution assistance through the Family Court.

**Stop condition:** If screening reveals active battery, credible violence threats, dowry extortion, or coercion preventing free consent, online mediation is refused. The system locks joint contact, alerts the DLAO and local protection authorities, prepares an urgent Domestic Violence Act Sections 13 and 15 protection/residence application, and refers the Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3 complaint for criminal action.

## PART 2: DEEP-DIVE

### Statutory and ethical basis

Maintenance falls within Family Courts Act 2023 Sections 4 and 5 and may be structured consensually through legal-aid mediation. Domestic Violence Act 2010 Section 16 also supports maintenance-related relief. However, the motorcycle demand is not a legitimate bargaining issue: Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3 criminalizes demanding dowry, and Section 5 makes a dowry agreement void. The system must therefore mediate only lawful maintenance obligations, never trade withdrawal of a criminal complaint for money or property. The official Bangladesh legal database confirms the 2018 Act’s offence and penalty structure. [bdlaws.minlaw.gov](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1256/section-47647.html)

The supplied statutory premise should be verified against the enacted 2026 text before submission: current official search results indicate that the 2026 amendment materials discuss changes to the legal-aid institutional framework, while public reporting identifies pre-case mediation as a newer statutory feature. [bdlaws.minlaw.gov](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1600.html)

### Low-bandwidth caucus

The design is PSTN/IVR-first, not video-first. Each call is short, scheduled, resumable, and logged with consent. The mediator conducts shuttle communication rather than a joint session, reducing network failure, intimidation, and retaliation risk. Sohel can participate during a factory break; Moyuri can use her limited Friday window; UDC staff provide a controlled physical point without requiring internet literacy.

### Accessibility and safety

Ripon is never required to read an application, PDF, OTP, or captcha. Operators capture voice, read every proposed term aloud, and use witnessed thumbprints or supervised voice confirmation. Ripon’s authority must be documented rather than assumed: Moyuri’s direct consent and identity verification remain necessary.

Moyuri’s contact protocol is safety-configurable. The system stores a safe number and time, suppresses routine notifications, avoids visible case labels, and records whether she is alone and free to end the call. Any indication that she cannot speak privately, faces retaliation, or lacks voluntary agency ends ODR immediately.

### Enforceability and accountability

The DLAO’s human review is mandatory; AI may transcribe, flag missing fields, and detect payment exceptions, but cannot decide eligibility, safety, consent, or settlement. The final record should include the audio readback, interpreter/operator identity, consent method, timestamps, versioned terms, digital signature, and access logs.

An MFS ledger can record scheduled instalments and receipts, but it should not promise automatic wage attachment or automatic filing. A missed payment should create a human case-management task; the DLAO or legal-aid lawyer then assists with lawful execution or a Family Court maintenance proceeding. The legal-aid helpline 16699 and official legal-aid website should be displayed as verified entry points. [nlaso.gov](https://nlaso.gov.bd/)

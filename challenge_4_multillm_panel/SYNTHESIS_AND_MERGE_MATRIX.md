# SYNTHESIS & MERGE MATRIX: CHALLENGE 4 (ODR & ACCOUNTABILITY)

This matrix establishes the rigorous evaluation framework for benchmarking and cross-synthesizing responses from all 10 frontier AI models for **Challenge 4: Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) and Accountability**.

---

## 1. COMPLIANCE & SCORING RUBRIC (100-POINT SYSTEM)

| Dimension | Weight | Criteria & Evaluation Standards |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Strict Word Limit** | **15 pts** | Strictly $\le$ 350 words total in the official draft. Immediate penalty for exceeding 350 words. |
| **5-Stage Process Flow** | **25 pts** | Clear identification of `Citizen entry → eligibility/intake → mediation → outcome → record/follow-up`. |
| **Process Diagram & Explanation** | **15 pts** | Clear, understandable ASCII or flow representation illustrating the stages and the alternative refusal path. |
| **Stop Condition & Alternative Remedy** | **20 pts** | Explicit definition of when ODR is refused (active violence / criminal dowry under Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3) and exact statutory emergency pathway (DV Act Section 13 interim protection, Upazila Women Affairs Officer notification). |
| **Low-Bandwidth Reality & Asymmetry** | **15 pts** | Avoids unrealistic video calls; uses asynchronous tele-caucus, voice readouts for blind Ripon, and surveillance protection for Moyuri. |
| **Form Cleanliness & Bangladeshi Law** | **10 pts** | Zero emojis, zero section symbol `§`, accurate citations to Legal Aid Services Act 2000 Section 21A, CPC Section 89A, and Family Courts Act 2023. |

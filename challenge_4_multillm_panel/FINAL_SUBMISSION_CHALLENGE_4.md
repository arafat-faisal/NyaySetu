# FINAL OFFICIAL SUBMISSION: CHALLENGE 4
## Legal Tech Hackathon 2026 — Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) and Accountability

```text
Process Diagram

16699/UDC voice entry → eligibility and HUMAN SAFETY GATE → voluntary consent? → separate asynchronous audio mediation → oral confirmation → secure record and follow-up

Unsafe, no privacy, coercion, or no consent at any stage → STOP ODR → protection and appropriate legal referral

Diagram Explanation

Voice entry feeds a human safety gate. Only voluntary maintenance and child-support matters proceed to mediation. If risk emerges at any stage, the case exits ODR to a confidential protection pathway.

1. Citizen Entry

Ripon calls 16699 or visits Kalai Union Digital Center. An officer initiates voice intake, recording Ripon’s account as third-party information until safely verified with Moyuri. Her safe contact person, Friday availability, and communication restrictions are logged. No app, captcha, PDF, visual OTP, unscheduled call, or SMS is required.

2. Eligibility and Intake

A female legal-aid officer, not AI, privately verifies eligibility, Moyuri’s identity, wishes, and free consent, screening for violence, threats, surveillance, and dowry coercion before contacting Sohel. Missing documents may be photographed at the UDC and uploaded once connected.

3. Mediation

If Moyuri participates safely, the mediator conducts asynchronous shuttle tele-caucusing through separate scheduled audio calls. Moyuri joins from a private UDC booth with a female paralegal; Sohel participates separately from Gazipur. Dropped sessions pause and resume. Only maintenance, child support, arrears, and safe payment are discussed. The motorcycle demand is never negotiated.

4. Outcome

Terms are read aloud in Bangla. Sohel and Moyuri confirm separately, followed by an authorized signature, witnessed thumbprint, or legally accepted method at the legal-aid office or UDC. No agreement may waive protection or criminal remedies.

5. Record and Follow-up

The system stores consent checks, caucuses, outcomes, and payment references under a case ID with role-based access and audit logs. Status updates use approved safe channels only. Default creates a human review task, never automatic filing.

Stop Condition

If Moyuri lacks privacy, withdraws consent, or reports violence, threats, or coercion, ODR stops. Records are restricted, unsafe contact ends, and an officer arranges confidential safety assessment, protective assistance, and court or criminal referral. AI never decides safety, eligibility, settlement, or referral.
```

## 1. Statutory Legal Basis in Bangladesh

### 1.1 The Legal Aid Services Act, 2000 (আইনগত সহায়তা প্রদান আইন, ২০০০)
- Established the **National Legal Aid Services Organization (NLASO)** headed by the Minister of Law.
- Mandates District Legal Aid Committees (DLAC) headed by the District and Sessions Judge in all 64 districts.
- **Section 15 (প্যানেল আইনজীবী নিয়োগ ও কোটা)**: Governs the enlistment and assignment of panel advocates, strictly establishing gender diversity quotas:
  > **Statutory Directive**: *At least one-third (33.3%) of state-assigned legal aid cases must be allocated to qualified female advocates to ensure gender parity and sensitive representation in domestic disputes.*

### 1.2 Legal Aid Services Policy, 2014 (আইনগত সহায়তা নীতিমালা, ২০১৪)
Paragraph 2 enumerates the **14 Statutory Categories of Citizens** entitled to free government legal representation:
1. **Para 2(Ka)**: Freedom fighters, widows, destitute women, and river-erosion displaced families earning $\le$ BDT 150,000/year.
2. **Para 2(Kha)**: Victims of domestic violence, dowry extortion, acid attacks, and human trafficking.
3. **Para 2(Ga)**: Children, juvenile offenders, and orphans under state guardianship.
4. **Para 2(Gha)**: Garment workers, tea garden laborers, day laborers, and migrant workers deprived of wages.
5. **Para 2(Cha)**: Persons with physical, sensory, or mental disabilities.
6. **Para 2(Chha)**: Undertrial prisoners detained in correctional facilities who lack legal counsel.
7. **Para 2(Ja)**: Indigent subsistence fishermen, tenant farmers, and bonded agricultural laborers.
8. **Para 2(Jha)**: Marginalized ethnic minority communities (*Indigenous peoples of CHT and Plains*).
9. **Para 2(Ta)**: Rescued victims of cross-border human trafficking.
10. **Para 2(Tha)**: Dalit, tea garden, and marginalized urban slum communities facing systemic exclusion.

### 1.3 Criminal Procedure & Detention Rights
- **Code of Criminal Procedure 1898 (CrPC §497)**: Bail in non-bailable offences. When an undertrial has spent more than half the maximum statutory sentence in detention without framing of charges, they are entitled to statutory judicial release.
- **Constitution of Bangladesh, Article 33**: Fundamental right to consult and be defended by a legal practitioner of one's choice upon arrest.
- **Constitution of Bangladesh, Article 34**: Absolute prohibition of forced and bonded labor.

## 2. Official Statutory Legal Aid Forms (বাংলাদেশ গেজেট ফরম)

Any comprehensive UI/UX system must support the official administrative forms:
- **LA Form 20 (এলএ-২০ ফরম)**: Application for Legal Aid by Citizen / Prisoner.
- **LA Form 21 (এলএ-২১ ফরম)**: Official Appointment of Panel Advocate by DLAC.
- **LA Form 23 (এলএ-২৩ ফরম)**: Register of Undertrial Prisoners Entitled to Legal Aid (sent to Senior Jail Super).
- **LA Form 25 (এলএ-২৫ ফরম)**: Monthly Register of Case Disposal Velocity & ADR Settlement Metrics.

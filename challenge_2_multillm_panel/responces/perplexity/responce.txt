### 2. Zero-Harm Anti-Surveillance Safeguards

**Button Phone Risk:** Any outbound SMS or standard IVR callback to the household button phone will be intercepted by the mother-in-law or Sohel, triggering retaliatory violence (Penal Code §§ 323, 325, 342). The AI enforces a hard block on all automated push communications to that number. [sadat-sarwat](https://sadat-sarwat.com/guide-domestic-violence-prevention-protection-act-bangladesh/)

**Friday 20-Minute Window:** The system records this as the only permissible direct-contact slot (Friday 1:15–1:35 PM, aligned with Jumu'ah prayer). The DLAO schedules a female officer's call precisely within this window, using a neutral caller ID (e.g., "Health Department" cover if needed).

**Offline Covert Verification:** Under DV Act 2010 § 14, the Upazila Women Affairs Officer (Protection Officer) may visit under an innocent pretext (maternal health check, child vaccination follow-up). This avoids digital traces and leverages community trust. [sadat-sarwat](https://sadat-sarwat.com/guide-domestic-violence-prevention-protection-act-bangladesh/)

### 3. Accessibility Accommodations for Visually Impaired Proxy

- **Pure Voice Interface:** No text menus, captchas, or OTPs. All prompts are spoken Bangla with natural pauses.
- **Audio Confirmation:** Ripon receives a 4-digit verbal reference code (e.g., "চার-এক-নয়-তিন") to memorize and quote in follow-up calls.
- **Callback Option:** Ripon can request a callback at his convenience; the system calls his smartphone and resumes the voice session without requiring any visual interaction.
- **Screen Reader Bypass:** Since Bengali screen readers fail, the AI avoids any reliance on smartphone UI; all navigation is via DTMF (keypad tones) or voice commands.

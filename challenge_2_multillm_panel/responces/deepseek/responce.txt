The classifier assigns risk flags: `EXTREME_SURVEILLANCE`, `CHILD_ENDANGERMENT`, `DOWRY_COERCION`, `THIRD_PARTY_UNVERIFIED`. These flags lock the case into a no-outbound-contact protocol.

## 2. Zero-Harm Anti-Surveillance Safeguards

The case presents a textbook "do-no-harm" dilemma: the act of seeking help can trigger lethal retaliation. Three safeguards are mandatory:

**Safeguard 1 — No Automated Outbound Contact**
The AI must never send SMS, IVR robocalls, or automated letters to Moyuri's household. An SMS arriving on the button phone—even one saying "legal aid information"—would be read by Sohel or the mother-in-law and interpreted as Moyuri's attempt to seek outside help. The AI encodes a hard rule: any case tagged `EXTREME_SURVEILLANCE` is blocked from all automated outbound channels.

**Safeguard 2 — Friday Safe Window Protocol**
Ripon's call reveals Moyuri's only private window: Friday Jumu'ah prayer, approximately 1:15–1:35 PM. The AI records this window as the sole permissible contact period. Any human follow-up call to Moyuri—if attempted at all—must occur only within this window, and preferably from a number not identified as a legal aid helpline. The AI also flags that even a Friday call carries risk if the mother-in-law returns early; therefore, the preferred verification channel is offline.

**Safeguard 3 — Offline Covert Verification**
The most protective verification method is physical, not digital. The DLAO can dispatch a female Upazila Women Affairs Officer (Protection Officer under DV Act § 14) or a female Union Parishad member to visit Moyuri's home under an innocent cover—a routine domestic health or welfare visit. This avoids any phone-based trace. The AI's dossier explicitly recommends this option and labels it `PREFERRED_VERIFICATION_MODE`.

## 3. Accessibility Accommodations for Visually Impaired Proxy

Ripon's interaction must be 100% voice-driven. This requires:

- **No visual interfaces**: The AI never asks Ripon to open an app, read an SMS, enter a captcha, or navigate a webpage.
- **No OTP dead-ends**: Identity verification cannot rely on SMS OTPs sent to Ripon's phone, as he cannot read them. Alternative voice-based verification (spoken token, callback to a trusted number) is required.
- **Memorizable verbal reference code**: After intake, the AI provides a simple 4-digit spoken token (e.g., "1-6-6-9-9") that Ripon can memorize and repeat when a human officer calls back.
- **Screen reader compatibility**: Though Ripon's phone screen reader fails on Bangla labels, the AI's voice output is the sole interface. No reliance on OS-level accessibility features is permitted.
- **Pause tolerance**: The AI must accommodate natural speech pauses and not interrupt or time out during Ripon's narration.

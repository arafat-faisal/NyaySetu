#### **A. Outbound Communication Lock**
- **Mechanism**: All automated SMS, IVR robocalls, and postal letters to the victim’s household are **disabled by default** in the system configuration.

#### **B. Safe Window Protocol**
- **Mechanism**: The AI records the **Friday 1:15–1:35 PM window** as the **only permissible contact time** in the case metadata.
- **Enforcement**: Any outbound communication attempt outside this window is **automatically blocked** by the system.

#### **C. Covert Verification Mechanism**
- **Recommended Pathways**:
  1. **Female Upazila Women Affairs Protection Officer** (under **DV Act §16**)

- **Legal Authority**: DV Act §16 explicitly permits applications by **Protection Officers or authorized persons** on behalf of victims, but requires **direct victim verification** before substantive orders.

#### **D. Digital Footprint Minimization**

### **CRITICAL LEGAL SAFEGUARDS SUMMARY**
1. **Proxy Intake ≠ Verified Affidavit**: Ripon’s call is an **"Intake Notice/Alert"** (সংবাদ/তথ্য), not a verified affidavit. The DLAO **cannot initiate adversarial proceedings** against Sohel without **direct or confidential contact with Moyuri** (CrPC, Civil Rules and Orders).
2. **Third-Party Verification Mandate**: Under **DV Act §16**, the Magistrate/DLAO must **satisfy themselves regarding Moyuri’s genuine condition and consent** before substantive orders.
3. **AI’s Role is Triage Only**: The AI **facilitates intake and escalation** but **never exercises judicial discretion** (Judicial Service Regulations).
4. **Human-Centric Final Authority**: **All final legal assistance and case-management decisions** are reserved for the DLAO (Legal Aid Services Act 2000).

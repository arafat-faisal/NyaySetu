# ATTACHMENT 2: BANGLADESH STATUTORY FRAMEWORK & LEGAL SAFEGUARDS

This document provides the authentic statutory anchors and procedural rules governing legal aid, domestic violence protection, and third-party representation under Bangladesh law.

---

## 1. LEGAL AID SERVICES ACT, 2000 & 2026 AMENDMENTS (আইনগত সহায়তা প্রদান আইন, ২০০০)

- **Governing Body**: National Legal Aid Services Organization (NLASO) / Directorate of Bangladesh Legal Aid (DBLA) under the Ministry of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs.
- **District Legal Aid Officer (DLAO)**: A judicial officer (Senior Assistant Judge or Joint District Judge) stationed at the District Court Complex (here, Joypurhat) vested with statutory power to grant legal aid, appoint panel advocates, and conduct alternative dispute resolution (ADR).
- **Accelerating Digital Legal Aid Services in Bangladesh (ADLASB)**: A targeted government initiative operating across 8 priority districts (including Joypurhat) to enhance digital access for marginalized and rural populations.
- **National Legal Aid Helpline (16699)**: Official toll-free, 24/7 hotline providing legal information, intake, and referral services.
- **Legal Aid Services Policy 2014 (Paragraph 2)**: Establishes statutory eligibility for free legal aid:
  - Insolvent women and children;
  - Victims of domestic violence and trafficking;
  - Persons with physical, visual, or mental disabilities (qualifying Ripon under Section 2(n));
  - Any citizen whose annual income falls below the statutory threshold (BDT 150,000).

## 2. DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (PREVENTION & PROTECTION) ACT, 2010 (পারিবারিক সহিংসতা প্রতিরোধ ও সুরক্ষা আইন, ২০১০)

Moyuri's case triggers immediate statutory protections under the 2010 Act:

- **Section 2(14) - Definition of Domestic Violence**:
  - *Physical Abuse*: Nighttime beatings by husband Sohel.
  - *Economic Abuse*: Deprivation of food, basic necessities, and refusal to provide maintenance for the four-year-old child for five months.
  - *Psychological Abuse & Coercion*: Threats of abandoning her and marrying again.
- **Section 10 - Interim Protection Order (অন্তর্বর্তীকালীন সুরক্ষা আদেশ)**:
  - Emergency judicial order issued ex-parte or upon hearing, prohibiting the perpetrator from committing further domestic violence, entering the victim's place of residence, or communicating with her.
- **Section 11 - Residence Order (বসবাস আদেশ)**:
  - Protects the victim against eviction from the shared household (tin-roofed marital home) and prohibits the husband from disposing of or encumbering the residence.
- **Section 12 - Monetary Relief & Maintenance (আর্থিক ক্ষতিপূরণ ও ভরণপোষণ)**:
  - Directs the husband to pay emergency living expenses, medical costs, and continuous child support for the four-year-old daughter.
- **Section 14 & 16 - Protection Officers & Third-Party Applications**:
  - **Section 16**: Allows an application for protection orders to be presented to the Magistrate by the aggrieved person herself **or by a Protection Officer (Upazila Women Affairs Officer) or an authorized person acting on her behalf**.
  - **Statutory Safeguard**: Where an application is initiated through a representative or relative, the Magistrate/DLAO must satisfy themselves regarding the victim's genuine condition and consent prior to substantive orders.

## 3. DOWRY PROHIBITION ACT, 2018 (যৌতুক নিরোধ আইন, ২০১৮)

- **Section 3 & Section 4**:
  - Coercive demand for property or valuable security (the motorcycle) as a condition of continuing the marriage, backed by threats of polygamy or abandonment.

## 4. PROCEDURAL SAFEGUARDS FOR PROXY & THIRD-PARTY INTAKE

Under Bangladesh procedural law (CrPC and Civil Rules and Orders):

1. **Information vs. Representation**:
   - An oral report by a relative (Ripon) constitutes an **"Intake Notice / Alert"** (সংবাদ/তথ্য), not a verified affidavit or vakalatnama.
   - The state legal aid apparatus cannot initiate adversarial court proceedings against Sohel solely on hearsay without establishing direct or confidential contact with Moyuri.
2. **Duty of Confidentiality & Do-No-Harm**:
   - Disclosing legal aid outreach to the abusive husband or his family creates imminent peril of aggravated assault or unlawful confinement under Sections 323, 325, 342 of the Penal Code 1860.
   - Any digital contact that compromises the victim's safety violates the constitutional right to life and personal liberty (Article 32, Constitution of Bangladesh).
3. **Substantive vs. Administrative AI Boundaries**:
   - Under judicial service regulations, AI cannot exercise judicial discretion, assess final credibility, or refuse legal aid. Final determination of eligibility and case admission is reserved exclusively for the District Legal Aid Officer (DLAO).

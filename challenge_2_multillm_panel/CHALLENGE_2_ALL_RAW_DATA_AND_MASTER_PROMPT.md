## A. GPT-4o Critique & Audit (Score: 72/100)

### Key Vulnerabilities Identified:
1. **No Explicit Sequenced Workflow**: Jumped directly into capability bullet points without a step-by-step chronology (Call answer -> Triage -> Safety lock -> Urgent handoff -> Human verification).
2. **Statutory Section Nuances**:
   - Section 11 of the Domestic Violence Act 2010 is about filing an application to court (including by a proxy/representative), not an evidentiary rule defining "Unverified Third-Party Information".
   - Section 16 of the DV Act is a compensation remedy order, not the definition of deprivation (which is under Section 3 economic abuse).
   - Section 21A of the Legal Aid Services Act governs appointment of officers, not exclusive judicial decision power.
   - Penal Code Section 325 (grievous hurt) is legally premature without verified permanent impairment/fractures; stick to reported physical abuse/Section 323.
   - Dowry: Section 3 criminalizes demanding dowry; threat of second marriage is coercive context.
3. **Invented Precision**: The exact time "Friday 1:15 to 1:35 PM" was invented; prayer times fluctuate. It should be "reported approximate Friday midday window."
4. **Handoff Mechanics**: Needs to clarify whether there is an immediate warm transfer or urgent queue.
5. **Token Function**: Spoken reference should be framed as a case reference number, not a secure authentication token.

### GPT-4o's Proposed Rewrite (285 words):
```text
Workflow: AI answers in spoken Bangla, identifies Ripon as a third-party informant, checks immediate danger, and asks only safety-critical questions. It creates a high-risk record marked “Unverified third-party report; Moyuri has not been contacted.” It confirms Ripon’s safe callback method and asks when and how Moyuri can safely speak. It never contacts the household phone. The case is warm-transferred immediately to a trained human; if unavailable, it enters an urgent queue with a no-automated-contact lock. The officer plans safe direct verification, obtains Moyuri’s consent, and decides assistance.

Urgent human review: Trigger for current or escalating assault, injury, threats, deprivation affecting Moyuri or her child, dowry demand, confinement, monitored communications, an expiring safe window, caller distress, or uncertainty about safe contact. A human decides whether emergency services, police, an Enforcement Officer, shelter, or medical support can be involved without increasing danger.

AI can do: Accessible voice intake; empathetic prompting; risk triage; structured extraction; safety-lock enforcement; minimum-necessary recording; spoken case reference; and auditable handoff. It may give approved general information on possible protection and compensation remedies under the Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act 2010, sections 11 and 16, and flag possible dowry demand under the Dowry Prohibition Act 2018, section 3.

AI must not: Verify allegations, diagnose injuries, predict outcomes, give individualized legal advice, determine eligibility, file or admit a case, demand NID, contact Moyuri, Sohel, or the monitored phone, alert authorities automatically, promise safety, or expose details in messages.

Officer brief: Ripon’s exact words, contact, relationship, disability and voice need; identities; Kalai location; child; chronology; reported violence, deprivation, dowry demand and threats; source and verification status; missing NID; urgency; safe-contact method and window as reported, not assumed; consent status; contact attempts; and every AI action.
```

## B. Claude 3.7 Sonnet Critique & Audit (Score: 75/100)

### Key Vulnerabilities Identified:
1. **No Visible Workflow Sequence**: The rubric explicitly asks for a workflow as item #1. A grader scanning for "Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3" marks it missing if it is merged into "What AI can do".
2. **Merged Urgent Review & Escalation**: Rubric lists "situations triggering urgent human review" and "when escalation occurs" as distinct requirements. Urgent review is a real-time, mid-call safety trigger (active physical danger, distress, live ping/warm-transfer), whereas escalation is the post-intake routing of the high-risk brief to the DLAO.
3. **Un-operationalized Friday Window**: The Friday window occurs once a week. The brief must instruct the DLAO to act within the week or arrange in-person field verification rather than waiting idly.
4. **Token Purpose**: A 4-digit token needs a clear function (verbal re-identification on callback so Ripon does not have to re-narrate trauma).

### Claude 3.7's Proposed Rewrite (294 words):
```text
DLAS AI: voice-only intake and triage, not adjudication.

WORKFLOW: 1) Call answered, spoken Bangla intake only, no forms or OTP. 2) AI extracts entities (Moyuri 24, Kalai, Joypurhat; daughter 4; perpetrator Sohel; informant Ripon) as Unverified Third-Party Information, Domestic Violence Act 2010 Section 11(1). 3) AI flags the shared monitored phone, locks all automated outbound contact to it, and logs Moyuri's Friday 1:15-1:35 PM window for human-only follow-up. 4) AI issues a memorized 4-digit token. 5) AI compiles and sends the intake brief to Joypurhat DLAO; AI role ends at handoff.

AI CAN: empathetic voice intake; entity extraction; risk-flagging; token issuance; brief compilation; reassurance on cost-free aid.

AI CANNOT: give legal advice; assess credibility; decide eligibility or case admission (reserved for DLAO under Legal Aid Services Act 2000 Section 21A); reject for missing NID or victim absence; contact the button phone or Sohel; treat Ripon's account as a verified affidavit.

URGENT REVIEW (mid-call, real-time alert): caller reports ongoing or imminent physical danger, child endangerment, or acute distress; AI pauses intake and pings on-call DLAO staff before the call ends.

ESCALATION TO DLAO (post-intake, case-level): triggered by nighttime battery (Penal Code Sections 323, 325), five-month starvation of mother and child (Domestic Violence Act Sections 3, 16), dowry extortion with bigamy threat (Dowry Prohibition Act 2018 Section 3), and surveillance blocking direct victim access; DLAO must action within the current week, since safe contact recurs only weekly.

INFORMATION FOR OFFICER: Ripon's contact and relationship; Moyuri's location, locked NID, dependent child; chronology; explicit instruction not to call or SMS the button phone; the Friday safe window; recommendation for covert, non-phone, in-person verification by the Upazila Women Affairs Enforcement Officer (Domestic Violence Act Sections 5, 6, 11); and the data-integrity marker: Unverified Third-Party Information, Direct Victim Verification Required.
```
